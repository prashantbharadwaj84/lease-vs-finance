When Leasing Makes Sense
  • Lower monthly payments fit your monthly budget better.
  • You prefer driving a new vehicle every 2 to 3 years with the latest technology and safety features.
  • You stay within standard mileage limits (e.g. 10,000 – 12,000 miles/year).
  • You don't want to worry about selling or trading in a depreciating vehicle at term end.
When Financing Makes Sense
  • You plan to keep the car long after loan payoff to enjoy years with zero monthly payments.
  • You drive high annual mileage and want to avoid excess mileage penalties ($0.15–$0.25/mile).
  • You want full ownership and the flexibility to customize or sell the car anytime.
  • You want to build long-term personal asset equity.
